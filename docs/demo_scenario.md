# Demo scenario: "Machine 4" (about 30 minutes)

Setting: a Korean-owned electronics plant near Ho Chi Minh City, day shift, Line 1 and Line 2.
Two people at one MindBridge unit: the **Supervisor** (blue button, English or Korean) and the
**Operator** (yellow button, Vietnamese). A **Technician** speaks from the supervisor side.

The script covers every judged capability: natural two-way conversation, technical terms and
numbers, a safety restriction with an understanding check, a contradicting order, recall questions,
an issue report, a resolved restriction, and the shift handover. Part B repeats the key moments in
VI ⇄ KO to show the multilingual goal.

**Delivery tips:** speak at normal pace from 20–40 cm, one or two sentences per turn, then pause.
Say numbers as words or digits naturally ("máy số 4", "Machine 4"). Leave each translation to play
before the next turn. Expected time per turn including speech: 25–35 s.

## Backup lines (if a turn is misrecognised)

Repeat the same sentence once, closer to the mic. If it fails twice, type it in the box under the
conversation (the judges see the same translation and memory behaviour) and say so on camera.
